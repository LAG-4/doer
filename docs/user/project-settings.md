# Spaces and saved details

A Space is a folder where related Tasks and files belong. Add a Space to work
with an existing folder, or start in My Stuff without choosing one.

Open **Settings → Spaces**, choose your Space, and fill in **About this Space**:
what it is for, your preferences, and details you want remembered. These details
apply to the next message and new Tasks in that Space, including reminders.
For example, a Job search Space can remember preferred roles and the name of
your resume file. A reporting Space can remember definitions and currency.

**Settings → General → Personal preferences** applies across Spaces on the
selected computer. Space details apply only within their folder. You can edit,
discard unsaved changes, or forget saved details at any time. Doer remembers
only what you choose to save here; attaching a document does not add its facts
to your saved details. Keep passwords and sign-in codes out of these fields.

Choose the computer and Space shown at the top of Settings before editing.
Settings for a disconnected computer cannot be saved until it reconnects.
Changes do not synchronize your folder or remembered details to other computers.

## Advanced settings

Advanced mode exposes provider configuration, keyboard shortcuts, source control,
remote connections, and developer tools. Ordinary Spaces do not require any of
these. Settings that support Space overrides can inherit the selected computer's
default or use a value saved for that Space.
