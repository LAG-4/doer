# Memories

Ask Doer to remember something and it keeps it for future tasks: “Remember that
I go by Sam” or “Remember that the garden budget is 4000.” Doer only remembers
what you ask it to — it never saves guesses, document content, or anything from
provider history on its own. Memories cannot keep secrets safe: never store
passwords, keys, or sign-in codes in them.

There are two kinds:

- **About you** applies across your Spaces on this computer, like your name or
  how you like summaries written.
- **This Space** applies to one Space only, like a budget, a deadline, or where
  something lives.

Ask in chat to review, correct, or forget anything: “What do you remember about
this Space?”, “The budget is 5000 now”, or “Forget the garden budget.” You can
also manage memories yourself: open **Settings** and look for **What Doer
remembers**, or open a Space's settings for its own section. Each entry shows
where it was saved; on web and desktop, entries saved in chat link back to that
task (mobile shows the source as text).

Saved memories are pulled into new messages in their scope, so corrections apply
going forward. When there is a lot to remember, Doer may see only part of what is
stored — ask it to list or search memories for the rest. Forgetting removes an
entry from future messages, but earlier conversation may still mention it.

Memories are stored on the computer hosting Doer and visible from connected
devices. They are included when recalled in requests to your chosen AI service;
there is no separate paid memory service.

Memories are individual facts ("What Doer remembers" in Settings). Standing
instructions that always apply — the About you / Preferences / Notes to always
include fields in Settings — live separately and are edited separately:
forgetting a memory never erases those notes, and clearing those notes never
forgets a memory.
